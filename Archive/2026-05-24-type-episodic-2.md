## Summary
Sentri Flutter — Project Overview. Version 1.7.2+37. Cross-platform Flutter app (iOS, Android, Web) for multi-business access control management. Target establishments: salons, spas, clubs, libraries, hostels — service businesses managing customer shifts, seat bookings, payments, and RFID card-based entry/exit. Manages: customers (profiles, shift history, payments, comments, images), seats (assignment per business), devices (ESP32 RFID hardware via LAN), cards (RFID assignment + blacklist), users (staff with role-based access), business (multi-tenant setup, expenses, subscriptions, WhatsApp templates). Flutter Material 3, Riverpod 2.x state management, GoRouter 13.x navigation, Firebase (Auth, Firestore, RTDB, Storage), Hive local cache, SharedPreferences. 149 Dart source files across 15 feature modules.
